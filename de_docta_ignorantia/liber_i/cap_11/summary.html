Incorruptible Certitude of Mathematical Entities in Apprehending Divine Truths — Structural Synopsis¶

Argumentative Architecture & Interactive Diagram Guide · DI I.11 (1440)

I. Argumentative Synopsis¶

  1. Ontological Status of Mathematical Entities (`DI I.11`): Visible things are symbolic mirrors of invisible realities (1 Cor. 13:12), yet sensible bodies fluctuate continually owing to material possibility. Mathematical entities, abstracted from sensible flux while retaining imaginable figure, possess incorruptible stability and certainty. Cusa marshals an unbroken canon—Pythagoras, Plato, Aristotle (“as the triangle is in the quadrangle, so the lower form is in the higher,” De Anima II.3, 414b29), Augustine, and Boethius—culminating in the mathematical refutation of Epicurean indivisible atoms via geometric divisibility.

  • Step 2 (Infinite Limit): Tangent horizon E–D–G absorbs expanding circles into coincident rectitude under intellectus.

  • Step 3 (Transsumptive Axis): Upper transcendent axis P–Q marks the leap beyond every spatial figure.
