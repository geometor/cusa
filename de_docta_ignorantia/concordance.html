Cross-Corpus Concordance¶

Linking Cusanus (1440–1459) to Ancient, Scholastic, Renaissance & Modern Geometry

Systematic concordance of 15 canonical loci across De Docta Ignorantia and Cusa’s Scripta Mathematica, cross-referenced to plato, aristotle, euclid/elements, archimedes, proclus, aquinas, hoenen, pacioli, durer, kepler, casey, klein, and wildberger.
