Paolo dal Pozzo Toscanelli
==========================

**Florentine Mathematician, Physician, Astronomer & Lifelong Friend of Cusa**

Fellow student of Cusa at Padua, advisor to Brunelleschi and Columbus, dedicatory recipient of De geometricis transmutationibus (1445), and interlocutor of Cusa's 1457 Dialogus de circuli quadratura; stood at Cusa's deathbed at Todi in 1464.
