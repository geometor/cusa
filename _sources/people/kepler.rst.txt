Johannes Kepler
===============

**Imperial Mathematician & Author of Mysterium Cosmographicum (1596)**

Hailed Nicholas of Cusa in Mysterium Cosmographicum Chapter II as 'divinus mihi Cusanus' explicitly for Cusa's transsumption of curved and straight as the archetypal symbol of Creator and created cosmos, and adopted Cusa's Principle of Continuity in Ad Vitellionem Paralipomena (1604) on conic foci at infinity.
