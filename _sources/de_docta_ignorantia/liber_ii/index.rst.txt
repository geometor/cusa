====================================
Liber II — De Universo et Cosmologia
====================================

.. rubric:: *De Docta Ignorantia · Liber Secundus* — Nicholas of Cusa (1440)

.. container:: title-page

   | **Nicholas of Cusa** (*Nicolaus Cusanus*, 1401–1464)
   |
   | *Liber Secundus: De Universo et Cosmologia*
   | Kinematic Relativity & Astrophysical Homogeneity

Book II develops the cosmology of the contracted universe. Because exact physical equidistance never occurs in nature, no celestial body can occupy an immobile geometric center, nor do fixed celestial poles exist. The universe is as though having its center everywhere and its circumference nowhere (*quasi habens undique centrum et nullibi circumferentiam*).
