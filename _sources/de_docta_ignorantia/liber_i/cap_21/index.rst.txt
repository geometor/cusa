Book I, Chapter 21 — Transsumption of the Infinite Circle to Unity
------------------------------------------------------------------

*Original GEOMETOR Translation from the Latin · h I, nn. 63–66 · Paris 1514 fol. 8v–9r · Rotta 1913 pp. 45–47*

.. _cusa-en-di-i-21-s-1:

.. folio:: DI I.21 §1

We have offered a few remarks concerning the maximal triangle; let us in like manner subjoin some reflections concerning the infinite circle:

.. _cusa-en-di-i-21-s-2:

.. folio:: DI I.21 §2

The circle is the perfect figure of unity and simplicity. Above it has already been shown that the triangle is a circle; thus trinity is unity. Now this unity is infinite, just as the circle is infinite; hence it is infinitely more one and more identical than any unity expressible or apprehensible by us. So great is the identity there that it precedes even all relative oppositions, since there 'other' and 'diverse' are not opposed to identity. Because the Maximum is of infinite unity, therefore, all attributes that belong to it are itself without diversity or otherness, so that its goodness is not one thing and its wisdom another, but the selfsame. For in it all diversity is identity; hence because its power is most one, it is also most strong and most infinite. So utterly one is its duration that within it the past is not other than the future, nor the future other than the present; rather they are one most unified duration, or eternity without beginning and end. So completely is beginning within it that within it even the end is the beginning.

.. _cusa-en-di-i-21-s-3:

.. folio:: DI I.21 §3

All this the infinite circle displays: eternal without beginning or end, indivisibly most one and most capacious. Because that circle is maximal, its diameter too is maximal. And since there cannot be several maxima, that circle is so supremely one that its diameter is its circumference. Now an infinite diameter has an infinite midpoint; and the midpoint is the center. Clearly, therefore, center, diameter, and circumference are identical. Thereby our ignorance is taught that the incomprehensible Maximum is that to which the Minimum is not opposed, and that within it the center is the circumference.

.. _cusa-en-di-i-21-s-4:

.. folio:: DI I.21 §4

You see how the whole Maximum exists most perfectly within everything simple and indivisible, because it is infinite center; outside all being, encompassing all things, because it is infinite circumference; and penetrating all things, because it is infinite diameter. Beginning of all things, because center; end of all things, because circumference; middle of all things, because diameter. Efficient cause, because center; formal cause, because diameter; final cause, because circumference. Bestowing being, because center; governing, because diameter; conserving, because circumference—and many similar truths besides.

.. _cusa-en-di-i-21-s-5:

.. folio:: DI I.21 §5

Through the intellect you apprehend, accordingly, how the Maximum is neither identical with nor diverse from anything, and how all things are in it, from it, and through it, because it is circumference, diameter, and center—not that it is a circle or circumference, diameter, or center, but solely the most simple Maximum, investigated through these paradigms and found to encompass all things that are and are not, so that within it non-being is maximal being just as the Minimum is the Maximum. And it is the measure of every circulation from potency into act and back again from act into potency, of composition from principles down to individuals and resolution of individuals back into principles, of perfect circular forms and circular operations and motions returning upon themselves and to their beginning, and of all like things whose unity consists in a kind of circular perpetuity.

.. _cusa-en-di-i-21-s-6:

.. folio:: DI I.21 §6

Much more concerning the perfection of unity might here be drawn from this circular figure, which for brevity's sake I pass over, since anyone can readily work it out in keeping with what precedes. One thing only I admonish you to note well: how all theology is circular and grounded in a circle, to such a point that even the names of the attributes are verified of one another circularly—supreme justice is supreme truth, and supreme truth is supreme justice, and so for all the rest. From this, should you wish to extend your inquiry, countless theological truths hitherto hidden will become utterly manifest to you.
