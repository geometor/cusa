===============
GEOMETOR • cusa
===============

.. rubric:: *De Docta Ignorantia et Scripta Mathematica* — Coincidence of Opposites, Infinite Figures & Exact Circle Rectification

Critical four-track Digital Scriptorium Book Codex and exact symbolic geometry suite uniting the mathematical, epistemological, and cosmological core of :person:`Nicholas of Cusa's <cusanus>` (*Nicolaus Cusanus*, 1401–1464) ***De Docta Ignorantia*** (**February 12, 1440**) with his four constructive mathematical treatises: ***De Geometricis Transmutationibus*** (**1445**), ***De Mathematicis Complementis*** (**1453**), ***De Mathematica Perfectione*** (**1458**), and the complete ***Aurea Propositio in Mathematicis*** (**August 8, 1459**).

Every Codex unit is presented across **four synchronized tracks** powered by ``photon-platform/publish``:

1. **English** — A freshly authored, 100% original GEOMETOR scholarly translation rendered directly from the primary Latin witnesses (124 synchronized paragraphs across 21 treatise sections).
2. **Latin (1440 / 1514–1913)** — Public-domain Latin collated from :person:`Paolo Rotta's <klibansky>` 1913 Laterza edition (restoring two *homeoteleuton* omissions in `I.13` and `I.14` verified on :person:`Raymond Klibansky's <klibansky>` annotated copy at McGill Library) and the 1514 Paris / 1565 Basel *Opera*.
3. **Summary** — Argumentative architecture, deductive structure, and interactive SVG diagram guides.
4. **Critical Notes** — Exact ``geometor.model`` symbolic analyses, Möbius circle-inversion and Rational Trigonometry proofs, Renaissance philology, and cross-corpus reception across :person:`Plato <plato>`, :person:`Aristotle <aristotle>`, :person:`Euclid <euclid>`, :person:`Archimedes <archimedes>`, :person:`Proclus <proclus>`, :person:`Aquinas <aquinas>`, :person:`Regiomontanus <regiomontanus>`, :person:`Kepler <kepler>`, :person:`Snellius & Huygens <snellius>`, :person:`Klein <klein>`, and :person:`Wildberger <wildberger>`.
