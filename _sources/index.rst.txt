================
Nicholas of Cusa
================

.. image:: _static/medallion.svg
   :alt: Nicholas of Cusa Medallion
   :class: repo-medallion-hero

The mission of this project is to capture a semantic digital library of the works of :person:`Nicholas of Cusa <cusanus>` (1401–1464), the Renaissance polymath and cardinal whose philosophical and mathematical writings pioneered the coincidence of opposites, infinite geometrical transformations, and centerless cosmology. Within the interconnected `GEOMETOR <https://geometor.com/books/>`_ semantic digital archive, these works are liberated from static print bounds—decomposed into addressable semantic units, calibrated vector diagrams, primary-source manuscript provenance, and cross-corpus citations speaking one shared geometric language.
