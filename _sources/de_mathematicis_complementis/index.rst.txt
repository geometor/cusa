============================
De Mathematicis Complementis
============================

.. rubric:: *Scripta Mathematica* (1445–1459) — Nicholas of Cusa

*De Mathematicis Complementis* and Cusa's companion *Scripta Mathematica* represent the cardinal's constructive geometric treatises composed between 1445 and 1459. Spanning isoperimetric polygons, circle rectification, the critical dissection of Archimedes' spiral, and the Golden Proposition (*Aurea Propositio*), these works apply the principles of learned ignorance to constructive geometry.
