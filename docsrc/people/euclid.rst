Euclid of Alexandria
====================

**Author of the Elements**

Supplies the geometric foundations of Cusa's demonstrations: triangle inequality (I.20), angle sum of two right angles (I.32), greater side opposite greater angle (I.18–19), tangent perpendicularity (III.16, III.18), inscribed polygons (Book IV), and mean proportional quadrature (VI.13; Cusa cites Campanus's numbering VI.9) (geometor/euclid, geometor/elements).
