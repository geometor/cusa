St. Thomas Aquinas
==================

**Scholastic Philosopher of Mathematical Abstraction & Judgment**

Articulated the threefold degree of abstraction and intelligible matter (In Boethium de Trinitate q. 5–6), against whose strict Aristotelian non-contradiction in finite ratio Cusa distinguishes higher intellectus (geometor/aquinas).
