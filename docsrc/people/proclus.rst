Proclus Diadochus
=================

**Neoplatonic Commentator on Euclid Book I & Plato's Parmenides**

Cusa owned and heavily annotated manuscripts of Proclus's Commentary on the Parmenides and Elements; Proclus's ontology of mathematicals in the imagination directly parallels De docta ignorantia I.11–12 (geometor/proclus).
