src.geometor.cusa.coincidentia_circle_line¶

Exact symbolic constructions and proofs for De Docta Ignorantia I.13 (Circle & Straight Line).

Models Nicholas of Cusa’s celebrated coincidence of the infinite circle and tangent arc with the infinite straight line (De docta ignorantia, Book I, Chapter 13):

  1. construct_tangent_circle_pencil(): Coaxial parabolic pencil of circles \(C_R: x^2 + (y - R)^2 = R^2\) (\(R \in \{1, 2, 4, 8\}\)) tangent to the horizon line \(y = 0\) at \(O = (0, 0)\), intersected by the secant lines \(x = \pm 1\) in exact quadratic radicals (\(1, 2-\sqrt{3}, 4-\sqrt{15}, 8-3\sqrt{7}\)).

  2. verify_sagitta_and_curvature_limits(): Exact SymPy symbolic rationalization of the sagitta \(h(x, R) = R - \sqrt{R^2 - x^2} = \frac{x^2}{R + \sqrt{R^2 - x^2}}\), proving \(\lim_{R \to \infty} h(x, R) = 0\), \(\lim_{R \to \infty} R \cdot h(x, R) = \frac{x^2}{2}\), and curvature \(\kappa(R) = \frac{1}{R} \to 0\).

  3. construct_circle_line_inversion() & verify_mobius_inversion_coincidence(): Exact Möbius circle-inversion proofs demonstrating both (a) inversion at \(O = (0, 0)\) mapping every circle \(C_R\) to the horizontal straight line \(y = \frac{1}{2R} \to 0\), and (b) bounded inversion in the unit circle \(\Omega\) centered at \(S = (0, -1)\) mapping every \(C_R\) to the rational circle \(C'_R\) centered at \(\left(0, -\frac{R}{2R+1}\right)\) of radius \(\frac{R}{2R+1}\), whose continuous limit \(C'_\infty\) at \(\left(0, -\frac{1}{2}\right)\) of radius \(\frac{1}{2}\) is identically the inverse image of the straight horizon line \(y = 0\).

Construct Cusa’s I.13 coaxial pencil of circles tangent to the straight line AB at O.

Builds horizon line [ A B ] (\(y = 0\)), tangency origin O = (0, 0), normal axis [ O C_max ] (\(x = 0\)), circles ( C_R O ) for each \(R \in \text{radii}\), and symmetric vertical secants \(x = \pm 1\) cutting the arcs in exact quadratic radicals \(P_R = (1, R - \sqrt{R^2 - 1})\) and \(Q_R = (-1, R - \sqrt{R^2 - 1})\).

Parameters:

  • radii – Strictly positive integer radii (default (1, 2, 4, 8)).

  • model – Optional existing Model.

Returns:

Populated Model with zero floating-point coordinates.

src.geometor.cusa.coincidentia_circle_line.construct_circle_line_inversion(radii: collections.abc.Sequence[int] = (1, 2, 4), model: geometor.model.Model | None = None) → geometor.model.Model¶

Construct the Möbius circle-inversion model unifying Cusa’s tangent circles and straight line.

Inverts the coaxial pencil \(C_R: x^2 + (y - R)^2 = R^2\) and the horizon line \(y = 0\) in the unit circle \(\Omega: x^2 + (y + 1)^2 = 1\) centered at \(S = (0, -1)\) passing through \(O = (0, 0)\):

  • Every finite circle \(C_R\) maps to the exact rational circle \(C'_R\) with center \(M_R = \left(0, -\frac{R}{2R + 1}\right)\) and radius \(r'_R = \frac{R}{2R + 1}\) tangent at \(O = (0, 0)\).

  • The straight horizon line \(y = 0\) maps to \(C'_\infty\) with center \(M_\infty = \left(0, -\frac{1}{2}\right)\) and radius \(r'_\infty = \frac{1}{2}\) passing through \(O = (0, 0)\) and the inversion pole \(S = (0, -1)\).

Parameters:

  • radii – Positive integer source radii (default (1, 2, 4)).

  • model – Optional existing Model.

Returns:

Populated Model over \(\mathbb{Q}\).

src.geometor.cusa.coincidentia_circle_line.verify_mobius_inversion_coincidence(radii: collections.abc.Sequence[int] = (1, 2, 4, 8)) → dict[str, Any]¶

Verify symbolically both Möbius inversion theorems for Cusa’s I.13 pencil.

  1. Origin Inversion at \(O = (0, 0)\): maps \(x^2 + (y - R)^2 = R^2\) to the horizontal line \(v = \frac{1}{2R}\), converging to \(v = 0\).

  2. Pole Inversion at \(S = (0, -1)\) in \(\Omega: x^2 + (y + 1)^2 = 1\): maps \(C_R\) to \(u^2 + \left(y' + \frac{R}{2R+1}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{R}{2R+1}\right)^2\), converging as \(R \to \infty\) to \(u^2 + \left(y' + \frac{1}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{4}\), the exact image of the straight line \(y = 0\).

Parameters:

radii – Sample integer radii for tabular output.

Returns:

Dictionary of verified symbolic identities and InversionCircleEntry rows.
