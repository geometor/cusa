src.geometor.cusa.polygon_exhaustion_and_complementa¶

Exact symbolic constructions for Polygon Exhaustion (I.3) and Cusa’s Scripta Mathematica (1445–1459).

Unites Nicholas of Cusa’s epistemological paradigm of polygon-in-circle incommensurability (De docta ignorantia I.3) with his constructive mathematical treatises:

  1. construct_polygon_exhaustion() & compute_polygon_exhaustion_table() (De docta ignorantia I.3 & De mathematicis complementis 1453): Exact inscribed and circumscribed regular \(n\)-gons (\(n \in \{3, 4, 6, 8, 12\}\)) in closed-form radicals, tracking Cusa’s prima linea (inradius \(r_n = \cos(\pi/n)\)), secunda linea (circumradius \(R_n = 1\)), and strictly monotone inscribed/circumscribed semi-perimeters bracketing transcendental \(\pi\).

  2. construct_transmutatio_isoperimetra() & verify_transmutatio_1445() (De geometricis transmutationibus, 1445, §§9–11): Cusa’s compass-and-straightedge quarter-side sesquiquarta (\(5:4\)) rectification of the inscribed equilateral triangle BCD in unit circle A, producing \(|AE| = \frac{\sqrt{7}}{4}\), isoperimetric radius \(|AH| = \frac{5\sqrt{7}}{16}\), and \(\pi_{\text{Cusa},1445} = \frac{24\sqrt{21}}{35} \approx 3.14233762\).

  3. construct_aurea_propositio() & verify_cusa_snellius_approximation() (De mathematica perfectione, 1458, & Aurea propositio in mathematicis, August 8, 1459): Exact \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3})\) construction of Cusa’s three-ray sector AB, AD, AC terminated by arc BDC, chord BHC, tangent EDG, and compensated segment IKL with \(AK = \frac{3\cos\theta}{2 + \cos\theta}\), together with the SymPy Maclaurin proof of fifth-order accuracy: \(\theta - \frac{3\sin\theta}{2 + \cos\theta} = \frac{\theta^5}{180} + \frac{\theta^7}{1512} + O(\theta^9)\).

Construct Cusa’s 1445 De Geometricis Transmutationibus (§§9–11) rectification.

In unit circle ( A D ) centered at A = (0, 0), inscribes equilateral triangle < B C D >, divides side [ B C ] into four equal quarters at E, F, G, draws ray [ A E ] of exact length \(\frac{\sqrt{7}}{4}\), and extends it in sesquiquarta ratio (\(5 : 4\)) to H with \(|AH| = \frac{5\sqrt{7}}{16}\), the radius of Cusa’s nearly isoperimetric circle ( A H ).

src.geometor.cusa.polygon_exhaustion_and_complementa.verify_transmutatio_1445() → dict[str, Any]¶

Verify Cusa’s 1445 sesquiquarta rectification and implied algebraic \(\pi\).

Since the inscribed equilateral triangle has exact perimeter \(3\sqrt{3}\) and Cusa’s isoperimetric circle has radius \(r_{\text{Cusa}} = |AH| = \frac{5\sqrt{7}}{16}\), equating \(2 \pi_{\text{Cusa}} r_{\text{Cusa}} = 3\sqrt{3}\) gives:

\[\pi_{\text{Cusa},1445} = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2 \cdot \frac{5\sqrt{7}}{16}} = \frac{24\sqrt{21}}{35} \approx 3.1423373949\]

exceeding true \(\pi\) by only \(7.447 \times 10^{-4}\) (relative error \(2.37 \times 10^{-4}\), well inside Archimedes’ upper bound \(22/7 \approx 3.142857\)).

Returns:

Dictionary of exact symbolic quantities and error bounds.

Construct Cusa’s 1458–1459 Aurea Propositio three-ray diagram over \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3})\).

Instantiates Aurea propositio in mathematicis (§§1–2) at half-angle \(\theta = \frac{\pi}{6}\) (\(30^\circ\)) so every point lies in exact \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3})\):

  • Center A = (0, 0), middle ray [ A D ] to D = (1, 0), outer rays at \(\pm 30^\circ\) through B = (\sqrt{3}/2, 1/2) and C = (\sqrt{3}/2, -1/2);

  • Circular arc ( A D ), chord [ B C ] meeting [ A D ] at H = (\sqrt{3}/2, 0), and tangent [ E G ] at D with E = (1, \sqrt{3}/3), G = (1, -\sqrt{3}/3);

  • Cusa’s compensated rectification segment [ I L ] through \(K = \left(\frac{3\cos\theta}{2 + \cos\theta}, 0\right) = \left(\frac{12\sqrt{3} - 9}{13}, 0\right)\), where \(|AI| + |AK| + |AL| = 3 = |AB| + |AD| + |AC|\) and \(|IL| = \frac{6\sin\theta}{2 + \cos\theta} = \frac{24 - 6\sqrt{3}}{13} \approx \text{arc}(BDC)\).

Parameters:

model – Optional existing Model.

Returns:

Populated Model over \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3})\).

src.geometor.cusa.polygon_exhaustion_and_complementa.verify_cusa_snellius_approximation() → dict[str, Any]¶

Prove Cusa’s three-ray identity and the fifth-order Maclaurin series residual.

Verifies symbolically in SymPy:

  1. Exact Chord–Tangent Three-Ray Invariance (Aurea propositio §1): \(\frac{|AB| + |AH| + |AC|}{|BC|} = \frac{2 + \cos\theta}{2\sin\theta} \equiv \frac{2\sec\theta + 1}{2\tan\theta} = \frac{|AE| + |AD| + |AG|}{|EG|}\).

  2. Fifth-Order Maclaurin / Padé Series (De mathematica perfectione & Aurea propositio): \(\theta - \frac{3\sin\theta}{2 + \cos\theta} = \frac{\theta^5}{180} + \frac{\theta^7}{1512} + \frac{\theta^9}{25920} + O(\theta^{11})\).

  3. Algebraic :math:`pi` Approximations at \(\theta = \frac{\pi}{4}, \frac{\pi}{6}, \frac{\pi}{12}\).

Returns:

Dictionary of verified symbolic expressions, series coefficients, and \(\pi\) bounds.
