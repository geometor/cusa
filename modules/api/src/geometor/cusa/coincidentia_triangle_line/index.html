src.geometor.cusa.coincidentia_triangle_line¶

Exact Rational Trigonometry constructions & proofs for De Docta Ignorantia I.14–15.

Models Nicholas of Cusa’s proof that the infinite triangle coincides with the infinite straight line (De docta ignorantia, Book I, Chapters 14–15):

Given fixed base endpoints \(B = (-1, 0)\) and \(C = (1, 0)\) (matching Cusa’s printed base bc in the 1514/1565/1913 diagrams of I.14) and flattening apexes \(A_n = \left(0, \frac{1}{n}\right)\) for \(n \in \mathbb{Z}_{>0}\):

  • Base quadrance \(Q_{BC} = 4\), equal side quadrances \(Q_{BA_n} = Q_{A_n C} = 1 + \frac{1}{n^2}\).

  • Pure rational base spreads \(s_B = s_C = \frac{1}{n^2 + 1} \in \mathbb{Q}\) and apex spread \(s_{A_n} = \frac{4n^2}{(n^2 + 1)^2} \in \mathbb{Q}\), satisfying Wildberger’s Triple Spread Formula identically and \(\lim_{n \to \infty} s_{A_n} = 0\) (as the obtuse apex angle approaches two right angles, \(180^\circ\)).

  • Wildberger’s Triple Quad Formula asymmetry \(\mathcal{Q}(Q_{BC}, Q_{BA_n}, Q_{A_n C}) = (Q_{BC} + Q_{BA_n} + Q_{A_n C})^2 - 2(Q_{BC}^2 + Q_{BA_n}^2 + Q_{A_n C}^2) = \frac{16}{n^2} = 16\,\text{Area}^2 \to 0\), vanishing identically when the triangle resolves into the simple straight line BC.

Construct Cusa’s I.14–15 family of flattening triangles over \(\mathbb{Q}\).

src.geometor.cusa.coincidentia_triangle_line.verify_triangle_line_coincidence() → dict[str, Any]¶

Prove Cusa’s I.14 triangle-to-line limit symbolically via Rational Trigonometry.

Establishes over \(\mathbb{Q}(n)\) for symbolic positive \(n\):

  1. \(s_A(n) = \frac{4n^2}{(n^2 + 1)^2}\) and \(s_B(n) = s_C(n) = \frac{1}{n^2 + 1}\) satisfy the Triple Spread Formula identically;

  2. \(\lim_{n \to \infty} s_A(n) = 0\) and \(\lim_{n \to \infty} s_B(n) = 0\);

  3. The Triple Quad Formula asymmetry equals \(\frac{16}{n^2} \to 0\);

  4. The side-excess \((|BA_n| + |A_n C| - |BC|) = \frac{2}{n^2\left(\sqrt{1 + 1/n^2} + 1\right)} \to 0\).

Returns:

Dictionary of symbolic invariants and limit checks.
